# Peter's 10–15 minute playtest

Use the current downloadable `dist/dr-manhattan.html`, a keyboard and mouse, and
**Normal** challenge with invulnerability off. Export your palace backup first.
A separate browser profile lets you try a fresh campaign without replacing your
usual Continue point. These checks sample two time targets and the Long Summer
opening; they cannot establish fairness for every level.

## 0–1 minute: understand the goal

- Open The Glass Palace, choose the Prologue pane and read its three goals.
- Pin a challenge. Can you explain what earns it and what would miss it without
  reading a technical guide? Note any unclear wording.
- If you have old fragments, check they remain recovered and are labelled as
  retained from earlier rules. Do not restore a backup for this check.

## 1–5 minutes: Prologue at your normal pace

- Play the Prologue normally. Read or skip dialogue as you ordinarily would;
  do not rush just because a timer is visible.
- Try the photograph and the named optional research interactions. Are they
  findable? Do their progress counters respond when you actually use them?
- Open Pause → Moment Challenges. Time should stop. Change the tracked goal and
  return to Pause, then Resume. Does the tracker leave objectives and E prompts
  readable?
- If you finish, record field time and the displayed target. Open H / Challenge
  Details. Do new, missed and legacy fragments make sense? If the window ends
  first, record where you stopped and why.

## 5–10 minutes: Reassembly

- Start from the beginning and gather/deposit atoms using normal controls.
- Try gathering some atoms without holding Coalesce. Can you understand the
  passive-gather goal, and can you tell when a vortex causes a loss?
- Is the six-minute target plausible at your pace, after learning the route?
  Record completion time if available, or current layer/time if unfinished.
- If you Retry, confirm the pane still explains that observed losses are kept.
  Retry must not silently turn a failed clean-run condition into a success.

## 10–13 minutes: Long Summer's opening

- Return to the title and open Bonus Levels → The Long Summer on Normal.
- Use the available tools deliberately: move into cover, try a nonlethal attack,
  teleport out of danger and use a duplicate when useful. Avoid testing balance
  only by standing still or wandering without responding to warnings.
- For roughly 60–90 seconds, note health lost, deaths, which attack caused trouble,
  whether warnings were understandable, and whether a safe recovery route exists.
- Try the tracker with City Heat. Is a brief delayed update distracting? Can you
  still read health, Heat, objectives and interaction prompts?

## 13–15 minutes: record the result

| Check | Record |
| --- | --- |
| Browser / build | Browser name, source or downloadable file |
| Prologue | Time or stopping point; target felt generous / fair / tight |
| Reassembly | Time or layer reached; target felt generous / fair / tight |
| Long Summer | Approximate health after 60–90 seconds, deaths, unclear attacks |
| Challenge clarity | Exact wording or object that was hard to understand/find |
| Tracker | Any overlap, misleading result or unexpected reset |

Mark a result **not checked** when you did not reach it. A single difficult
opening or one slow first attempt is useful feedback, not enough by itself to
justify changing all enemy damage or every time target.
