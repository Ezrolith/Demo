# After Midnight

Three masks. One city. A different kind of hero.

Choose **AFTER MIDNIGHT** from the title screen. The original Manhattan campaign
and its three bonus adventures remain in their existing menus. The new cases
are available immediately, without finishing the original story.

## Choose your hero

| Hero | Approach | Signature tools |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Nite Owl | Armour, distance and preparation | Stun bolts, a physical grapple, chaff smoke, investigative visor and a shock ring |
| Silk Spectre | Fast combinations and timing | Returning baton, vault, moving feint, timed counter-guard and a sweeping kick |
| Rorschach | Close pressure and observation | Heavy strikes, a pulling hook, smoke, reading an opponent's weakness and intimidation |

These heroes are human. Damage, cover, stamina and positioning matter. Their
traversal follows a physical route and stops at walls; it is not Manhattan's
teleport. Attacks knock people out rather than unmaking them.

WASD/arrows move; the mouse aims; **C / left mouse** fights; **Q / right mouse**
uses secondary gear; **Space** traverses; **R** deceives or conceals; **F / Shift**
uses the hero's focus/guard; **G** uses the signature crowd-control move.
**E** interacts, **H** recalls the current hint, **Esc / P** pauses and **M** mutes.
The case board's **HERO CONTROLS & TACTICS** explains each loadout.

## Case files

- **Dead Air:** June Vale hears her own voice on a channel she has never used.
  Follow three intercepted traces across the rainy roofs, triangulate the
  listening network and recover its buyer list.
- **Undertow:** a storm, a leased cargo pier and four workers written into a
  contract as cargo. Restore the winch and physically escort them to the lifeboat.
- **The Glass Archive:** a museum exhibition built from the private lives of
  people who called for help. Unpick its security grid and confront the Surveyor.

Every case has three checkpoint stages and can be played with any of the three
heroes. Dialogue acknowledges the selected character. Hero-specific checkpoints
let you experiment without replacing another character's progress.

## Replay modes

**Night Patrol** sends paced waves into your chosen location. Amber rings warn
of incoming enemies. Between cleared waves, use E at camp to choose one gear
upgrade, take the field kit, continue or extract. Armour improves health,
capacitors improve stamina, and mobility improves movement/traversal. Each
upgrade has three levels. Extraction banks a successful patrol; death records
your score, and retry begins a fresh run with fresh gear.

**Rescue Clock** gives you 180 seconds to find four civilians and guide them to
the green safe passage. Each actual rescue adds eighteen seconds and score.
Nearby gunmen frighten escorts into stopping; clear their route. Reinforcements
and amber strike warnings keep the street dangerous. You can extract after a
rescue, but rescuing all four is the full objective. Running out of time is
recorded honestly, rather than counted as a win.

Both modes use three distinct locations: **Rain over North Borough**, **East
River Storm**, and **The Glass Archive**. Personal bests are kept per hero,
mode and location.

## Progress and backups

After Midnight has its own save slot. It does not change your campaign Continue
point, bonus checkpoints, moral totals or endings. Settings are shared.
Story checkpoints remember the selected hero. Live challenges restart from
the beginning; they do not pretend to resume a paused timer or unfinished wave.

Use **BACK UP / RESTORE PROGRESS** on the case board to download a JSON backup.
Choosing a file previews it; Restore is a separate action and replaces only
After Midnight progress. Keep an export before restoring. The old Bonus Levels
backup is separate and continues to cover the original three bonus adventures.
If browser storage fails, the board warns that progress is session-only; export
before closing.

Keyboard and mouse/trackpad are required. All graphics, weather, music and sound
remain procedural and use the original game's renderer and synthesizer.
