# The Glass Palace

Choose **THE GLASS PALACE** after New Game on the title screen. Jon can walk
through the palace on Mars without combat. Use **WASD / arrows** to move,
**E** to read or enter a nearby moment, and **Esc / P** to leave.

## Moments

The six campaign chapters, three bonus adventures and three After Midnight
cases each have a pane. Campaign panes follow the existing chapter unlocks;
the optional adventures and cases remain available under their normal rules.
Each pane draws a small moving view using that level's own map code.

Approach a pane and press E for **Play**, **Resume checkpoint**, or **Challenges**.
Play starts that level normally. Resume uses its existing saved checkpoint.
After Midnight uses the hero selected in its case board; the moment menu names
that hero. Existing campaign and adventure menus remain available from the title.

## The glass ledger

Read the central slab to see spared and unmade totals across all three existing
save slots. Its reflection changes through four tones as the totals change.

The ledger follows what each slot actually retains: campaign totals, the latest
completed bonus adventures, and the latest saved After Midnight records. Replays
follow those slots' existing rules. This is a combined view of saved outcomes,
not a reconstructed count of unique people or every historical attempt.

Walking through the hub, reading panes, viewing challenges and watching its
epilogue do not write those three slots. Launching a level resumes normal
gameplay and its normal progress writes.

## Thirty-six fragments

Each moment has three optional challenges: finish without unmaking anyone,
finish under its time target, and complete a level-specific objective. Examples
include all six memories, a complete evidence trail, or six residents rescued
with City Heat below 30. The challenges are listed at each pane.

Challenges are detected when a level actually completes. Each can be earned once;
you can collect missing challenges on later attempts. Existing completed levels
unlock their moments but do not silently award new challenges retroactively.
End-of-level screens show **0–3 / 3 moments**. Each new challenge contributes a
glass fragment, and the palace's central structure grows toward thirty-six.

Palace challenges and epilogue history have their own save slot. If saving is
blocked or a newer palace save is protected, the hub displays a warning. Session
progress can remain visible even when it has not been saved; enable browser
storage before relying on it after closing the game.

## Earth

Complete the six campaign chapters and all three bonus adventures to reveal a
new Earth moment in the hub. After Midnight completion and all thirty-six
fragments are not required for this scene.

The reflection takes about ninety seconds at the normal reading pace and has
three versions selected by the combined ledger. Slower reading settings extend
it. Space reveals narration, the existing dialogue controls remain available,
and Escape returns to the palace. The ending is recorded in the palace slot
only after the reflection finishes.
